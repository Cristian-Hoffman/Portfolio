## Project Overview

The **Liftoff Training Dashboard** was developed to solve a critical bottleneck in the **XWings FPV** team: the time-consuming process of manually tracking pilot improvement. By automating the extraction and visualization of data from the **LiftOff drone simulator**, the team can now monitor performance in real-time.

The primary goal of this dashboard is to **reduce training time frames** by identifying specific areas where pilots need improvement (such as specific turns or lap consistency) and providing immediate feedback.

### Key Benefits

- **Consistency Tracking**: Monitors lap time variance to ensure pilots are not just fast, but reliable.
- **Automated Reporting**: Replaces manual spreadsheets with an automated data pipeline.
- **Progress Visualization**: Clear graphs show pilot evolution over weeks and months of training.
- **Performance Benchmarking**: Allows the team to set target times for specific tracks and easily identify who is race-ready.

## How it Works

The dashboard integrates simulation data to provide a comprehensive view of:
*   **Average Race Position**: Historical ranking across various simulated events.
*   **Lap Time Analysis**: Detailed breakdown of sector times to find where milliseconds are being lost.
*   **Training Volume**: Tracking the number of races and hours spent in the simulator to correlate effort with results.
