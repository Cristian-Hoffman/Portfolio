## Project Overview

**XWings FPV** is the official drone racing team of Universidad Nacional de Colombia, representing the university at national and international FPV (First Person View) racing competitions. Founded in April 2019, we are a joint initiative between IEEE UN, CEIMTUN-RAS (Comité de Estudiantes de Ingeniería Mecatrónica UN - IEEE RAS), and AESS UN (Aerospace and Electronic Systems Society - IEEE AESS).

We are a passionate team of students and researchers dedicated to competing at the highest level on tracks around the world, combining technical expertise with piloting skills to push the boundaries of drone racing.

I joined the team in early 2020, and since 2023 I've served as the chair, leading the technical and logistical aspects of the team, as well as representing the team at competitions and events.

### Mission

Our mission is to:

- **Compete**: Participate in national and international drone racing competitions
- **Innovate**: Develop and optimize custom racing drones
- **Educate**: Train new pilots and technical team members
- **Represent**: Showcase UNAL's talent in robotics and autonomous systems
- **Collaborate**: Work with IEEE, RAS, and AESS to promote drone technology

## What is FPV Drone Racing?

**FPV (First Person View) drone racing** is a high-speed competitive sport where pilots navigate racing drones through complex obstacle courses while wearing video goggles that provide a real-time view from the drone's onboard camera.

### Key Characteristics:

- **Speed**: Racing drones can reach speeds of 120+ km/h
- **Agility**: Advanced maneuvers including flips, rolls, and split-S turns
- **Precision**: Navigate through gates and around obstacles with millimeter accuracy
- **Immersion**: Pilots experience flight as if they were in the cockpit
- **Technology**: Custom-built drones with cutting-edge electronics and aerodynamics

## Skills & Technologies

### Piloting Skills

- **Manual Flight Control**: Acro mode flying with no stabilization
- **Racing Line Optimization**: Finding the fastest path through courses
- **Freestyle Maneuvers**: Advanced tricks and aerial acrobatics
- **FPV Navigation**: Spatial awareness through video goggles
- **Emergency Recovery**: Handling crashes and equipment failures

### Technical Skills

- **Drone Assembly**: Building custom racing quadcopters from components
- **Soldering & Electronics**: Circuit board assembly and repair
- **Flight Controller Configuration**: Betaflight tuning and setup
- **PID Tuning**: Optimizing flight characteristics for performance
- **Video System Setup**: FPV camera and transmitter configuration
- **Battery Management**: LiPo charging, storage, and safety
- **Aerodynamics**: Frame design and propeller selection
- **Troubleshooting**: Diagnosing and fixing technical issues

### Software & Tools

- **Betaflight**: Flight controller firmware and configuration
- **Betaflight Configurator**: GUI for flight controller setup
- **Blackbox Explorer**: Flight log analysis
- **FPV Simulator**: Training software (Liftoff, DRL Simulator, etc.)
- **CAD Software**: Custom part design for 3D printing
- **Video Editing**: Competition footage and promotional content
